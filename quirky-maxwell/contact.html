Frequently Asked Questions
What is CinePulse Movie Portal?
CinePulse is a movie information web portal developed for the course Web Technology–I (2301CS363) at Darshan Institute of Engineering & Technology. It showcases clean semantic HTML5 markup, responsive CSS3 layouts, and accessibility best practices without relying on external CSS frameworks or JavaScript.
How can I suggest a new movie for inclusion?
You can use the inquiry form above and select "Suggest a New Movie" in the category dropdown. Please mention the movie title, release year, director, and why it should be featured in our curated international catalog.
How do favorites and watchlists function?
In this Phase-1 static frontend, the Favorites page displays curated bookmarked titles with runtime statistics, status filters ("Plan to Watch" vs "Watched"), and quick links to watch high-definition trailers.
How are movie reviews published?
Community members can submit reviews using the form on the Reviews page. Each submission includes a star rating, user details, and written critique adhering to the CinePulse community rating guidelines.
